## Design Context

### Users
One person: Bami (@itsbami_), a UK Business Analyst career creator. He opens this tool on his phone between meetings to approve, hold or reschedule cross-posts, and on his Mac once a week to check the calendar and fix failures. Speed to a decision matters more than richness.

### Brand Personality
A faithful mirror of the Repurpose.io console, which Bami already uses and knows by heart. Friendly SaaS, not a Swiss timetable. Three words: **familiar, tidy, reassuring**.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Reference**: the Repurpose.io app (my.repurpose.io), explored logged-in on 3 Sep 2026. Everything below was measured there.
- **Theme**: light. Page background `#f7f8fa`, white cards with a 1px `#e5e7eb` border and 12px radius, no shadows except on pop-up menus.
- **Sidebar**: 220px, white, 1px `#ededed` right border, brand at top, a full-width pink pill button beneath it, icon + label nav items, active item `#e3e4e6` with 10px radius, avatar and name pinned to the bottom. On phones it becomes an off-canvas drawer behind a top bar.
- **Accent**: pink `#ff6680` for primary buttons only. Green `#22a559` on `#e8f8ee` for healthy and active. Blue `#007bff` on `#eef6ff` for scheduled. A purple gradient switch (`#6d5df6` to `#8a7bff`) for Auto Publish. Red only for failures.
- **Fonts**: DM Sans at 14px for everything, JetBrains Mono only for IDs and error text.
- **Components**: filter bar with a bordered search box and white dropdown buttons, 66x88 thumbnails with a dark duration badge, one pink action button per content row, kebab menus for secondary actions, tabbed settings (Publish, Content, Destination, Source), connection cards in a grid, calendar in a card with a Month/Week/Day segmented control and a pink circle on today.
- **Anti-references**: dark mode, glows, gradient text, side-stripe accents.

### Design Principles
1. **If Repurpose.io has a pattern for it, use that pattern.** Bami should never have to learn a second console.
2. **Status is still the product.** Every row shows a status pill and the next time something happens.
3. **One pink button per row or card.** Everything else is white, ghost, or in the kebab menu.
4. **Errors are first-class.** Full error text, monospace, inline, plus a red notice on every page while a connection is unhealthy.
5. **Phone first for decisions.** Publish now, Release and Re-add are full-width on a 375px screen.
