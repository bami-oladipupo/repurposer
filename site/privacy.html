Repurposer

Privacy policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

Repurposer is a private tool operated by Bamiyo Oladipupo ("the operator") to repost his own TikTok videos to his own YouTube and Instagram accounts. It has one user: the operator. This policy explains what data the tool touches and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

Repurposer uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using it, the operator agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data obtained through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the tool accesses

  • YouTube. Through the YouTube Data API, the tool uploads videos to the operator's channel and reads the channel's name and ID to confirm which channel is connected. It requests the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes.
  • Instagram. Through the Instagram API, the tool publishes Reels to the operator's account and reads the account's username and publishing limits.
  • TikTok. The tool reads the public listing of the operator's own TikTok profile and downloads the operator's own videos.

How the data is used

Access is used solely to publish the operator's own videos to the operator's own accounts, on a schedule the operator sets. The tool does not read viewer data, comments, analytics, subscriber lists, or any content belonging to other people.

Where the data is stored

Everything runs on a computer owned by the operator. Access tokens, downloaded videos and the publishing log are stored locally on that computer and nowhere else. No data is sent to any third party other than TikTok, Google and Meta as needed to perform the download and upload.

Sharing and selling

No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking services in the tool or on this website.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Repurposer's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Downloaded videos are kept locally until they have been published, then removed. The tool stores YouTube data only as far as needed to operate: the connected channel's name and ID, the IDs of videos it has uploaded, and the OAuth token. It does not store any other YouTube data and keeps nothing for longer than 30 days beyond the record of what was uploaded. The operator can delete all stored data by deleting the tool's local data folder.

The operator can revoke the tool's access to YouTube data at any time through the Google security settings page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, and Instagram access in the Instagram app under Apps and Websites. After revocation the tool can no longer act on those accounts and any stored token stops working.

Cookies and tracking

Neither the tool nor this website sets cookies or uses any tracking, analytics or advertising technology. The tool does not serve ads and does not use YouTube data for advertising in any form.
