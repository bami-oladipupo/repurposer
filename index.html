Repurposer

A private, self-hosted tool that reposts one creator's own TikTok videos to their own YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels accounts.

What it does

  • Watches a single TikTok account for new videos.
  • Downloads each video and checks its length and format.
  • Uploads it to the creator's YouTube channel as a Short, and to their Instagram account as a Reel, with a caption built from the original TikTok caption.
  • Publishes on a schedule the creator sets, and reports every success and failure to the creator.

Who uses it

Repurposer is operated by Bamiyo Oladipupo for his own accounts only. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

Google account access

Repurposer asks for permission to upload videos to YouTube and to read basic channel details. It uses this access only to publish the creator's own videos to the creator's own channel. Details are in the privacy policy and terms of service.
